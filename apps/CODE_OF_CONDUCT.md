# Code of conduct

RustEDA Studio is a technical project that welcomes contributors of every background and level of
experience.

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent, ask before assuming, and accept correction gracefully.
- Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not tolerated in any project
  space, including issues, reviews and chat.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation of anyone who violates
these rules. Report concerns privately to the maintainers; reports are handled confidentially.
